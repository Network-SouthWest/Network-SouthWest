We make bots.

Here we make the discord bots you know and love. The beginnings of Networker Bot - The railway themed discord bot, began in January 2025 as a fun project and has now grown across 30+ servers and over 180 individual users. This year, LTR bot was debuted - being the ultimate utility bot for the LTRTrains community, running #gif-war, #photo-gallery, and #bidding-war, making the day to day operation of the server simpler, as well as consistently delivering the features loved by the community.
